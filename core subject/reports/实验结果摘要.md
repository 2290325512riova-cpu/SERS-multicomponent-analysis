### 3.1 Spectrum-KAN 定向审计

目的：先确认 G2=0.480 是否为可复现的模型行为，以及是否由增强/mixup 单独造成。

| 条件 | p1 G2 Macro-F1 | raw G2 Macro-F1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| no_aug | 0.467±0.128 | 0.514±0.065 |
| aug_no_mixup | 0.472±0.052 | 0.472±0.045 |
| composition_mixup | 0.480±0.037 | 0.588±0.065 |

关键结论：

- `composition_mixup/p1` 与原 benchmark 的 Spectrum-KAN p1 六任务结果完全一致，说明原 G2=0.480 不是回传/同步错误。
- no_aug 与 aug_no_mixup 都没有救回 G2；raw+composition_mixup 有提升但仍只有 0.588，远低于 classical ML。
- 增强效果是任务依赖的：p1 上 G3 从 no_aug 0.614 提升到 composition_mixup 0.891，说明不能简单说增强“完全无效”；但它不能解决 Spectrum-KAN 的 G2 失配。

## 6. 关键观察与讨论点

- **G2 困难来源**：Fig5 的 MG 1616 压制为核心谱学证据，说明 G2/MG 分级困难来自竞争吸附诱导的 MG 特征峰衰减与混合物峰区重塑；预处理、模型容量和增强消融作为辅助解释。
- **MG 单独存在准确率 95%**，MG+MBA 双混降至 35.9%——这一显著下降正是 MBA 共存竞争吸附干扰 MG 信号的直接证据，与 Fig5 的 1616 压制相互印证。
- **三混中"全不同浓度"子集准确率 83.9%**，"全同浓度"仅 17.5%
- **预处理选择**：p4（Cosmic+SG+ALS，不含 SNV）对 MG 相关任务更友好

## 9. 2026-05-25 Model Locking And Optuna Decision

Current manuscript route:

- Formal Optuna is skipped. The benchmark is framed as a standardized cross-model comparison with fixed, reproducible model configurations, not as exhaustive hyperparameter search.
- ExtraTrees/p1 is locked for downstream TreeSHAP and soil presence validation because ExtraTrees has the highest overall benchmark mean (0.971) and the best G2/p1 result (0.966±0.009).

- RamanNet-Lite remains the strongest DL-family comparator and augmentation-ablation member, not a practical winner or optimization star.
- These downstream evidence blocks have since been completed: six-task ExtraTrees/p1 TreeSHAP, SHAP-guided feature selection, and formal soil-matrix screening validation are now the active paper evidence.
